# Pic2Link Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Effective date / 生效日期: 2026-09-05

## English

Pic2Link is a macOS utility by Li Wei for uploading images and files to storage services that you configure. Pic2Link does not operate an upload relay, require a Pic2Link account, include advertising or analytics SDKs, or track you across apps or websites.

### Images, files, and captions

Pic2Link reads clipboard images and files to show an upload preview. Finder or Photos selections are read when you request a direct upload. Optional caption layout, resizing, and Live Photo GIF conversion happen on your Mac. Original images are not modified. Paired Live Photo video resources may be downloaded from iCloud into a temporary task folder and are removed when the task finishes. Other in-memory processing results are not saved as separate image files.

When you confirm an upload, Pic2Link sends the selected image or file directly from your Mac to the provider you configured. The provider receives the content, filename, required authentication, and connection information such as your IP address. It processes this information according to its own policies and your storage settings. Original file metadata may remain in an unmodified upload. A public image URL may allow anyone with the link to access the uploaded content; configure your provider accordingly. Removing a local history item does not delete a remote object.

### Local storage and clipboard

Provider passwords, access keys, and tokens are stored in macOS Keychain. Profile settings, upload history, thumbnails, saved captions, language preferences, and granted folder bookmarks are stored locally. You can remove profiles and clear history in the app. Pic2Link writes nonblank submitted captions and completed upload links to the clipboard according to your choices. Other apps and clipboard managers may read clipboard content.

### Permissions

Notifications report upload results. Automation permission reads your selection in Finder or Photos only when you request it. Photos permission reads selected library resources. The Mac App Store build may ask you to grant read-only access to a photo folder and remember that permission with a sandbox bookmark. Optional launch at login uses macOS login items. You can manage system permissions in System Settings.

### Support and control

The developer does not receive the files you upload through Pic2Link. If you email support or file a public GitHub issue, the information you choose to include is received through that service and is used to respond to your request. Do not include credentials or private photos in public issues. Contact **wallyvay@gmail.com** for privacy questions or deletion of information you directly sent to support. Third-party storage data and Apple purchase/account records are managed by their respective providers.

Pic2Link is a one-time paid app. Third-party storage plans and charges are separate. This policy may be updated to reflect product changes; the effective date will be revised.

## 简体中文

Pic2Link 是由 Li Wei 开发的 macOS 工具，将图片和文件上传到您自行配置的存储服务。Pic2Link 不提供上传中转服务器、不要求注册 Pic2Link 账号、不包含广告或分析 SDK，也不跨 App 或网站跟踪用户。

### 图片、文件与文案

Pic2Link 读取剪贴板中的图片和文件以展示上传预览，仅在您发起直接上传时读取 Finder 或“照片”中的选中内容。文案排版、尺寸压缩、实况照片转 GIF 均在 Mac 本机完成，不修改原始图片。实况照片配对视频可能需要从 iCloud 下载到任务临时目录，任务结束后删除；其他内存处理结果不会另存为图片副本。

确认上传后，图片或文件由 Mac 直接发送到您配置的服务商。服务商会收到上传内容、文件名、必要的身份认证信息及 IP 地址等连接信息，并按照其隐私政策和您的存储配置处理。原样上传的文件可能保留原始元数据。公开链接可能允许任何持有链接的人访问内容，请合理配置存储权限。删除本地历史不会删除云端文件。

### 本地存储与剪贴板

图床密码、Access Key 和 Token 存入 macOS Keychain。图床配置、上传历史、缩略图、文案、语言偏好和已授权文件夹书签保存在本机。您可以在 App 内删除图床配置或清空历史。按您的操作，Pic2Link 会将提交的非空文案及上传完成的链接写入剪贴板；其他 App 或剪贴板管理工具可能读取这些内容。

### 权限

通知用于报告上传结果。自动化权限仅在您请求时读取 Finder 或“照片”的选择；图库权限用于读取选中的资源。Mac App Store 版可能请求只读访问照片所在文件夹，并用沙盒书签记住此授权。开机启动为可选的系统登录项。您可在系统设置中管理相关权限。

### 联系与控制

开发者不会收到您通过 Pic2Link 上传的文件。如果您发送支持邮件或提交公开 GitHub Issue，您主动提供的信息会经相应服务交给开发者，用于处理请求。请勿在公开问题中填写密钥或私人照片。隐私问题或删除您主动发送给支持团队的信息，请联系 **wallyvay@gmail.com**。第三方存储数据、Apple 购买及账号记录由相应服务商管理。

Pic2Link 为一次付费买断 App；第三方存储套餐与费用另计。政策随产品变化更新，并同步修订生效日期。
